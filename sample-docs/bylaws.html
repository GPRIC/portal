Bylaws (Amended 2025)

1 · Board Of Directors

The board has three to nine directors serving two-year terms. Officers are a President, a Treasurer and a Secretary.

2 · Meetings

The board meets at least quarterly. A majority of directors in office is a quorum.

3 · Conflicts Of Interest

A director with an interest in any matter must disclose it and step out of the discussion and vote. The minutes record who stepped out.

4 · Spending On Behalf Of Affiliated Teams

  1. A team submits a written request with amount, purpose and pay-to.
  2. The board votes; interested directors step out.
  3. Once paid, receipts are due within 60 days.
  4. A team with overdue receipts may not submit new requests.
  5. Section 4.6: the board may vote by email when all directors receive the motion.

5 · Amendments

These bylaws may be amended by two-thirds of directors in office.
