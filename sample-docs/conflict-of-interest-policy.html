Policy · Adopted March 18, 2025

Conflict Of Interest Policy

Purpose

To protect GPRIC when it considers a transaction that might benefit a director, officer or their family.

Duty To Disclose

An interested person must disclose the interest before the board discusses the matter.

Stepping Out

The interested person leaves the discussion and does not vote. The Secretary records who stepped out in the minutes.

Annual Statements

Each director signs a yearly statement confirming they have read and follow this policy.
