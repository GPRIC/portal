Policy · Adopted March 18, 2025

Gift Acceptance Policy

Gifts We Accept

  1. Cash, checks and online gifts through Zeffy.
  2. Gifts restricted to a named team fund or the Equipment Fund.
  3. In-kind equipment the Treasurer confirms we can use and store.

Gifts Needing Board Approval

Gifts over $10,000, gifts of real estate or securities, and any gift with unusual conditions.

Donor Privacy

Donor names are shown publicly only with the donor's permission. Teams see totals only.
